              Keyboard shortcut Hold to talk, or a key combination
              
Hold Fn, speak, then release to transcribe. Choose a preset or record another shortcut, then save.

            

            

              Type in other apps Optional
              
Paste each finished dictation into the text field you’re using in another app. Your words also stay in Murmur’s draft.

              Mac access & recovery Optional access, help with permissions
              
    Changed access but still having trouble? Recheck, then quit and reopen Murmur. On a managed Mac, your administrator may need to allow access. You can keep using the draft and Copy.
    Access says allowed, but still does not work?
    First recheck, then quit and reopen Murmur. If access still fails, remove and re-add Murmur in the affected System Settings privacy pane, selecting the exact app you are opening now. Older builds may appear under the previous name, dicktaint. Try the shortcut in another app again after reopening.
